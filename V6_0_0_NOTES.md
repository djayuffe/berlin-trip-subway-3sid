# Subway 3SID v6.0.0 — Berlin composition rebuild

The scheduler was stable, but the previous Berlin patterns were nearly static A-note loops.
The tune has now been rewritten as a coherent A-minor arrangement.

## Berlin A
Progression: Am – F – G – Am

- sparse offbeat arpeggios
- restrained four-on-the-floor drums
- final-bar fill
- bass resolves into each following chord

## Berlin B
Progression: Am – C – G – F

- denser bass movement
- denser 16th-note hats
- stronger final-bar fill
- returns naturally from F to Berlin A / Am

## 3SID
- SID1 `$d400`: bass, sub and ghost fifth
- SID2 `$d420`: triad arp, fifth harmony and shimmer
- SID3 `$d440`: kick, clap/snare and hats

## Timing
- stable 7 frames per row
- 64 rows per section
- one beat every four rows
- arp phases 0, 2 and 4
- effects remain bar-locked

## Mix
- kick sidechain shortened to seven frames
- bass ducks more than the melodic SID
- accent kick uses nine-frame envelope
- smoother bass and arp ADSR
